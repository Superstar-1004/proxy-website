Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact support?

Reach us via live chat in the dashboard, email at support@proxyvault.com, or join our Discord community. Support is available 24/7.

Do my proxy credits expire?

No. All purchased traffic on ProxyVault is non-expiring. Use your bandwidth whenever you need it.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password" on the login page or contact support with your account email for assistance.

What is your refund policy?

Unused traffic can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. Monthly ISP and datacenter plans are refundable within 24 hours if no proxies were used.

Why are my proxies not connecting?

Check your authentication credentials, ensure your IP is whitelisted if required, and verify you're using the correct gateway and port. See our troubleshooting guide in Documentation.
