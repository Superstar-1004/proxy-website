Frequently Asked Questions

What makes mobile proxies different from residential?

Mobile proxies route traffic through real mobile carrier networks (4G/5G), giving requests a mobile ASN and device fingerprint. Residential proxies use household ISP connections. Mobile IPs are often trusted more by social platforms and mobile-first services.

Can I choose a specific carrier?

Yes. Enterprise plans support ASN-level targeting to route through specific mobile carriers. Monthly and daily plans include automatic carrier selection within your chosen country.
