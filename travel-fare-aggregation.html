Frequently Asked Questions

Why do travel sites block scrapers?

Airlines and OTAs use anti-bot systems to prevent fare scraping and protect pricing strategies. Residential proxies reduce detection by routing traffic through legitimate household IPs.

Can I target specific departure cities?

Yes. Use country, state, and city-level geo-targeting to simulate searches from specific departure markets and compare regional pricing.
