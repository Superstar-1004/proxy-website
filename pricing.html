No. All purchased traffic and bandwidth on ProxyVault is non-expiring. Use it whenever you need it — there are no monthly reset cycles for pay-as-you-go plans.

Yes. You can purchase and use multiple proxy types from a single account. Each product has its own balance, and you can top up any product independently.

Is there a free trial?

New accounts receive a small trial allocation to test our proxies before committing. No credit card is required to create an account and start testing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and cryptocurrency. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice with NET-30 terms.

Do you offer refunds?

Unused traffic can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. Monthly subscription plans for ISP and datacenter proxies are refundable within 24 hours if no proxies have been used.
