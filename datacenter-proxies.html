Frequently Asked Questions

When should I use datacenter proxies?

Datacenter proxies are ideal for speed-critical tasks where IP trust score is less important — such as software testing, bulk scraping of unprotected sites, price monitoring, and infrastructure health checks.

Are there any thread or connection limits?

No. ProxyVault datacenter proxies have zero thread limits. You can run as many concurrent connections as your infrastructure supports.

Can I rotate IPs automatically?

Yes. Configure rotation intervals from per-request to custom time-based schedules through the dashboard or API.

What is the difference between datacenter and ISP proxies?

Datacenter proxies offer the fastest speeds and lowest cost but have moderate IP trust scores. ISP proxies provide higher trust scores with static IPs at a slightly higher price point.

Do plans include unlimited bandwidth?

Yes. All datacenter proxy plans include unlimited bandwidth with no overage charges for the duration of your subscription.
