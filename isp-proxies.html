Frequently Asked Questions

What are ISP proxies?

ISP proxies are static IP addresses assigned by Internet Service Providers but hosted on datacenter infrastructure. They offer datacenter-level speed with the trust score of residential IPs.

Can I renew my proxies?

Yes. You can manually renew or enable auto-renewal for any ISP proxy before its lease expires. Renewed proxies keep the same IP address.

Is bandwidth really unlimited?

Yes. ISP proxy plans include unlimited bandwidth with no throttling or overage charges for the duration of your lease period.

Which locations are available?

ISP proxies are currently available in the United States and major European markets, with APAC expansion underway. Check the dashboard for the latest subnet availability.
